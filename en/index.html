SIEM + SOC

A SIEM without analysts only makes noise

The SIEM collects and correlates events. Our SOC turns them into decisions: what is real, what is urgent and what to do.

  1. 01

    Collection

    We connect firewalls, servers, endpoints, Microsoft 365, cloud and critical applications.

  2. 02

    Correlation

    The SIEM normalizes events and applies detection rules and threat intelligence.

  3. 03

    SOC analysis

    An analyst reviews every alert, discards false positives and investigates the scope.

  4. 04

    Response

    We tell you what happened and the steps to follow, or contain the threat if we have agreed so.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us

Do I need to already have a SIEM to hire the SOC?

No. We provide the SIEM platform and manage it. If you already have one, we can work on top of it.

Is it only for large companies?

No. We design the service for small and medium-sized businesses, connecting only the sources that add value to keep costs reasonable.

Can pentesting affect my systems?

Tests are planned with you, with an agreed scope and schedule, and carried out in a controlled way so as not to interrupt your business.

Does it help with NIS2 or GDPR?

Yes. The monitoring, log retention, training and reports we deliver are useful evidence for complying with these regulations.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of devices, users and sources to monitor. After the free assessment we send you a fixed-price proposal.
