Managed SIEM + SOC

A SIEM collects and correlates the logs of your network, servers and applications. A SOC is the team of analysts who interpret what the SIEM detects. With this service you get both pieces: the platform and the people who watch it.

Request information View all services

Who it is for

Businesses that want to detect incidents in time but cannot staff a security team around the clock, or that need monitoring evidence for audits and regulations.

What it includes

  • Deployment and configuration of the SIEM platform, or work on the one you already have
  • Connection of the sources that add value: firewalls, servers, endpoints, Microsoft 365, cloud and critical applications
  • Detection rules tuned to your environment and reviewed regularly to reduce false positives
  • Analysis of every relevant alert by a SOC analyst
  • Notification with context and recommended steps, or containment agreed in advance
  • Log retention for audits and compliance

What you get

  • Monthly report on activity, incidents and trends
  • Report on each relevant incident with cause, scope and recommendations
  • Service level agreement with defined response times
  • Regular follow-up meeting

How we do it

Four steps, no surprises

1

Scope

Frequently asked questions

About this service

Do I need to already have a SIEM?

No. We provide the platform and manage it. If you already have one, we can work on top of it.

What happens when there is an incident?

An analyst investigates and notifies you through the agreed channel, with what happened and what to do. If you hire it, we can also contain the threat.

Does it help with NIS2 or GDPR?

Monitoring and log retention are useful evidence for both, together with other measures your company must apply.
