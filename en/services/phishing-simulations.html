Phishing simulations

Email is still the most common entry point for attacks. We run authorized simulated phishing campaigns to measure how many people fall for them and to strengthen both their training and the technical barriers.

Request information View all services

Who it is for

Businesses that want to know their real exposure to email or messaging deception, improve staff awareness, or meet security training requirements.

What it includes

  • Design of campaigns tailored to your company and its departments
  • Controlled sending and measurement of opens, clicks and data entered
  • Immediate training message for anyone who falls for the simulation
  • Analysis of real phishing attempts aimed at your company
  • Review of technical email and messaging protection measures

What you get

  • Results report per campaign and per department
  • Comparison between campaigns to see progress
  • Technical and procedural recommendations
  • Training proposal to strengthen weak points

How we do it

Four steps, no surprises

1

Agreement

We set objectives, dates and who participates.

2

Preparation

We create the scenarios and the sending infrastructure.

3

Campaign

We launch the simulation and measure behavior.

4

Reinforcement

We deliver results and propose training and improvements.

Do staff need to be told?

We decide together. Usually management and IT know, and staff do not know the date.

How often is it repeated?

Several campaigns a year show whether awareness is improving.
