Awareness training

A large share of cyberattacks begin with human error. We train the people in your company with real examples and practical exercises, adapted to the role of each group.

Request a quote View all services

Who it is for

Businesses that want to reduce human risk, meet training obligations, or give their technical and management teams a solid foundation in security.

What it includes

  • Introductory session for all staff
  • Workshops for middle managers and executives
  • Technical training for IT teams
  • Phishing prevention and good password practices
  • Personal data protection (GDPR)
  • Safe browsing and response to real incidents

What you get

  • In-person or online sessions with supporting material
  • Practical exercises and real cases
  • Learning assessment
  • Attendance report for your records

Yes, and it is the most effective: the simulation measures and the training reinforces.
