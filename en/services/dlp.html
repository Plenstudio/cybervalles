Data loss prevention (DLP)

Information is also lost from the inside, through error or on purpose. A DLP solution gives visibility into how your sensitive data moves, detects improper outflows and helps you respond, and it reduces the impact of ransomware.

Request a quote View all services

Who it is for

Businesses that handle personal data, intellectual property or critical information and want to control who moves it, how and where to.

What it includes

  • Monitor: visibility and control of how information is used
  • Detect: alerts on accidental leaks or theft attempts
  • Respond: preventive actions and support for regulatory compliance
  • Policies tailored to data type and each department
  • Deployment on devices with minimal impact on daily work

What you get

  • Configured data protection policies
  • Alerts and incident dashboard
  • Periodic reports for management
  • Support to document compliance measures

How we do it

Frequently asked questions

About this service

Does it monitor what my employees do?

It controls the movement of sensitive information according to the agreed policies. Staff should be informed and this reflected in the internal policy.

Does it slow down daily work?

It is deployed in phases and tuned to avoid unnecessary blocking.

Does it help against ransomware?

It reduces the impact by detecting abnormal data movements, together with backups and other measures.
